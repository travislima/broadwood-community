What this site collects

This website has no forms and does not ask you for any personal information. Joining the association, requesting to be added to a WhatsApp group, or making a donation all happen off this site — over WhatsApp, email or your bank — directly with the association.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this page — for example which sections are read and which buttons are used — so we can improve it. Clarity uses cookies and collects usage information such as clicks, scrolling, pages viewed, and general device and location information. It produces anonymised heat­maps and session replays; any text you might type is masked by Clarity by default. This information is processed by Microsoft on our behalf — see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may ask us what personal information we hold about you and request that it be corrected or deleted. In practice this website holds none — but for any privacy question, including about information you have shared with the association directly, contact us at bcramembership@gmail.com.

You can also limit analytics cookies through your browser's settings or by using private browsing.
